# Sudachi

The media logger of the citrus family: books, comics & manga, games, anime, films and series.

- **Today** — a pick for today planned around your shift (Mandarin), the shelf with one-tap progress, this week's counts, "still on this?", up next, comfort picks, discover, goals.
- **Library** — the collector's catalogue: every kind and status, search, filters, poster wall or list.
- **Backlog** — the brain dump (one title per line, any kind), Steam's pile to sort, the backlog ranked by fit.
- **Review** — every month and year as a Wrapped-style story, stats, your taste, your S–F scale, a year poster.
- Imports: Citrus, IMDb, Goodreads, MyAnimeList, Steam (the last two through your own Apps Script relay).

Vanilla ES modules, no build step, served by GitHub Pages from `docs/`. `bash test/all.sh` before every deploy, then `sh scripts-bump.sh`.
